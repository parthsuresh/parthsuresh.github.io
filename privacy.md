# Privacy

parthsuresh.com is a personal static website built with Jekyll and hosted on GitHub Pages, with Cloudflare in front. It has no accounts, no comments, no newsletter signup, no contact form, and no store. There is nothing here to log into and nothing here to purchase.

This site does not load Google Analytics, Cronitor, Pirsch, Openpanel, Cloudflare Web Analytics, or Simple Analytics. Cookie consent is off because the pages themselves do not set an analytics or advertising cookie. The hosting and CDN operators (GitHub and Cloudflare) may record ordinary request metadata such as IP address, user agent, and URL as part of serving and protecting the site. This site does not sell that information and does not use it to build an advertising profile.

Content on this site is meant to identify Parth Suresh as a researcher and to list publications. It is not a product API. Machine-readable copies live at `/llms.txt`, `/index.md`, and `/publications.md`. See [contact](https://parthsuresh.com/contact/) for why there is no public inbox or phone number, and [about](https://parthsuresh.com/about/) for who this site is about.
