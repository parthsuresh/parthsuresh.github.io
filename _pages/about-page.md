Parth Suresh is a Member of Technical Staff at [Datology AI](https://www.datologyai.com/), where he works on synthetic data generation for web-scale and long-context training. This page is the long-form about page for [parthsuresh.com]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/). The homepage at the apex domain is the same person, with selected publications and news.

Previously he was a Software Engineer (Machine Learning) at Meta Reality Labs, working on synthetic data, data curation, and benchmarks for language and multimodal models, including in egocentric and wearable settings. Before that he was an ML Research Engineer at Scale AI, focused on reasoning, synthetic data generation, and LLM judges. Earlier at Meta he worked at the intersection of developer productivity, software engineering, and machine learning.

The question he keeps coming back to is how we turn messy, real-world data into reliable signals for training and evaluating large models.
