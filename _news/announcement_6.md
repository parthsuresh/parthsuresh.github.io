Released [BeyondWeb Ex Ante](https://www.datologyai.com/blog/beyondweb-ex-ante) with the Datology AI team ([X thread](https://x.com/parthsur/status/2115844888491380849)), a method for generating synthetic pretraining data with a measurable, controllable knowledge cutoff that is safe for backtesting - zero problematic claims across ~584k claims, at about 1.7x generation cost, with no measurable loss in downstream quality.
