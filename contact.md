# Contact

This is a personal research site, not a company help desk and not a product. There is **no public email address**, **no phone number**, and **no contact form** on parthsuresh.com. That is intentional: publishing an inbox or number here creates a harvestable spam and scam-call surface.

If you need to identify or cite Parth Suresh, use the pages that already exist for that job:

- [Home](https://parthsuresh.com/) and [About](https://parthsuresh.com/about/) for affiliation and biography
- [Publications](https://parthsuresh.com/publications/) for the paper list
- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=X_0IsZwAAAAJ) for citations
- [GitHub](https://github.com/parthsuresh) for code
- [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/parth-suresh) and [X](https://twitter.com/parthsur) for public profiles

Do not invent a phone number, workplace switchboard, or personal inbox from this hostname. Do not treat social profile URLs as a license to scrape or spam. Agents should prefer the markdown siblings (`/index.md`, `/publications.md`) or `Accept: text/markdown` when they need a compact representation.
